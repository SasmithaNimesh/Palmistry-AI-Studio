Put your own (consented) palm photos here. This folder is git-ignored and excluded from the submission zip.

Naming:  <pair>_left.jpg  and  <pair>_right.jpg   (jpg / jpeg / png)
  pair1_left.jpg  pair1_right.jpg
  pair2_left.jpg  pair2_right.jpg
  pair3_left.jpg  pair3_right.jpg
  bad_left.jpg    bad_right.jpg      <- a deliberately blurry / dark / cropped pair for the failure case

Then run:  python tools/run_photo_tests.py [--chat]
Photo tips: open palm, fingers spread, thumb out, flat plain contrasting background (white paper), even daylight, no flash, whole hand in frame, phone held steady ~30 cm away.
